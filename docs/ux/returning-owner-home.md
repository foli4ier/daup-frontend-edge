# Returning owner hub

Signed-in owner who already has a house.

- Lands on **Places** after email. Does **not** open **Create your company / place**
- **Context** is the house name, city, and email — no protocol chrome
- **Open.** on the bound place is the first tap (terracotta)
- **Your places.** plus **On the chain.** live on Places
- **Get apps.** lives on Apps — LIVE: Eatery, EatOut, Project. Held: **Open.** only. Not held: one **Get.** as primary. Never both.
- Bottom thumb nav: **Places / Apps / You**
- **You.** shows **Log off.**, then **Settings.** (**Register a new house.** / **Delete the house.** / **Ask for an enhancement.**), then Advanced
- **Advanced** stays protocol-only, from You

Wizard opens only from **Register a new house.** or **+ Register**.
